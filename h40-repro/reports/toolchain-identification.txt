==============

  CLANG_TRIPLE=aarch64-linux-gnu-
  CROSS_COMPILE=aarch64-linux-android-
  CROSS_COMPILE_ARM32=arm-linux-androideabi-

  GNU assembler/binutils and GNU ld

the compiler an Android-NDK Clang 10.0.7 but contains no revision. The exact
binary was not found in public OnePlus/AOSP inputs.
